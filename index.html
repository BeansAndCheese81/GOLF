How To Play
1Pick a club from the bag. Longer clubs go further but curve more if you mishit.
2Drag on the course to aim, or tap Swing to fire straight at the pin.
3Tap once to set your power, tap again to time the strike. Land in the green zone for a pure hit.
4Rough saps distance and worsens your strike. Sand is brutal unless you pull the sand wedge.
5Wind is shown top of screen — it pushes shots off line, more so with lofted clubs.
6On the green, drag to putt. Watch the slope arrow — it'll pull your ball off a straight line.
